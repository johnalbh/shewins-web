# Editing the She Wins website

A one-page guide for Agnieszka and Sophia.

## Sign in

1. Go to **https://www.shewins.es/keystatic**.
2. Sign in with your email (Keystatic Cloud). The first time, accept the invitation email you received.

You will see three sections on the left: **Blog articles**, **Authors** and **Contact and links**. Events are not here: they are published on Meetup (see below).

## Publish a blog article

1. **Blog articles** → **Add**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title (English)** and **Title (Spanish)**. The web address is created from the English title. Do not change it after publishing (links and Google use it).
   - **Summary** in both languages, max 155 characters. It appears in the blog list and in Google results.
   - **Author** and **Publish date**. Newest articles appear first.
   - **Article (English)** and **Article (Spanish)**. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, lists, quotes and links. The **+** button adds three special blocks:
     - **Pink box**: a highlighted note or invitation.
     - **Checklist**: put a bullet list inside; each item gets a red tick.
     - **Sources**: put a bullet list of links inside; it is shown at the end as "Sources" / "Fuentes".
3. Click **Save**. The site updates in about **1–2 minutes**.

## Add an event

Events are published on **Meetup**, in the She Wins group, and the site reads them from there. Every event section of the site comes from Meetup: "Next free event", "This month", "Recent events" and "Past events".

1. Create the event on Meetup as usual.
2. For it to look right on the site:
   - **Title**: the name, then a colon and the subtitle, for example `CLARIDAD BAJO PRESIÓN: Conversaciones difíciles, decisiones que se retrasan`. The site shows the name as the title and the rest below it.
   - **Speaker**: in Meetup's speaker field, not only in the description. Without one, the site says "Community session".
   - **Price**: in Meetup's payment settings. An event without a price counts as free and can appear as the "Next free event".
   - **Image**: Meetup's 16:9 event image. The site shows it whole.
3. The site picks it up at its next update: every night, or after any save in this panel.

The language is worked out from the event's text, since Meetup has no field for it. You don't need to do anything when an event is over: every night the site moves it to "Past" by itself, and the next one becomes the "Next free event".

## Change an email or social link

**Contact and links** → edit → **Save**. It changes everywhere on the site (footer and links).

## Share the Letter

The She Wins Letter has its own page, made to be shared on Instagram, WhatsApp or by email:

- English: **www.shewins.es/letter**
- Spanish: **www.shewins.es/es/letter**

People subscribe there and choose whether they want the Letter in English or in Spanish. The site also invites visitors with a small card, and every blog article ends with a link to this page.

## Good to know

- Every save is kept in the history, so nothing is lost. If something looks wrong, tell John and he can bring back the previous version.
- Images: use JPG or PNG. The site resizes them automatically, but please avoid files over 5 MB.
- Text that is not a blog article or an event (home page, about, membership, legal pages) is changed by the developer. Send the new text to John.
